# Organic Content Engine

Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts. This extends the earlier content strategy doc (published separately as a Claude Docs page — link it here once you have it handy) with a structured concept bank organized by the categories requested, each with a full production spec.

For the weekly posting cadence, repurposing workflow, hashtag bank, and platform-specific mechanics (algorithm behavior, caption style per platform), see the earlier content-engine reference doc. This file is the **concept bank** — the actual list of things to film.

Every concept below has: Hook, Gameplay, Text overlay, CTA, Suggested duration.

---

## CHALLENGES

1. **"Bet you can't clear this in one try"** — Hook: on-screen dare. Gameplay: one hard-band level, uncut. Text overlay: "1 TRY. GO." CTA: "Duet your attempt." Duration: 10–15s.
2. **"Rating every mini-game type"** (recurring series) — Hook: "Rating every challenge type in this game." Gameplay: one clip per mechanic family (tap/memory/motion/precision/puzzle). Text overlay: difficulty rating per clip (e.g. "Tap: 6/10"). CTA: "Which one's hardest for you?" Duration: 20–30s.
3. **"Beat my score"** — Hook: final score reveal first, then how it happened. Gameplay: full run highlights. Text overlay: score counter. CTA: "Beat this." Duration: 15–20s.
4. **Weekly community challenge** — Hook: "This week's challenge: beat level 15 without losing a life." Gameplay: your own attempt. Text overlay: challenge rules. CTA: "Stitch your attempt." Duration: 15s.
5. **Speed-clear challenge** — Hook: "Clearing this in under 5 seconds." Gameplay: fastest clean clear you can capture. Text overlay: live timer. CTA: "Think you're faster?" Duration: 10s.

## RAGE MOMENTS

6. **Last-life fail** — Hook: "POV: level 14 has personally wronged you." Gameplay: real last-life death. Text overlay: lives counter hitting 0. CTA: none needed (pure entertainment, occasional "link in bio"). Duration: 8–12s.
7. **Fail compilation** — Hook: "3 fails, 3 different ways to lose." Gameplay: 3 quick different-mechanic fails cut together. Text overlay: "Fail #1 / #2 / #3." CTA: "Which is worse?" Duration: 15s.
8. **Rage-quit bait** — Hook: "Do NOT play this if you rage easily." Gameplay: a genuinely tough puzzle/memory challenge fail. Text overlay: none, let it speak. CTA: "Try it, I dare you." Duration: 10s.

## FAILS

9. **Misread fail** — Hook: "I read the instructions wrong and it cost me everything." Gameplay: a `color_match`/`correct_order`-style misread. Text overlay: "Read the prompt. Actually read it." CTA: none. Duration: 10s.
10. **Timer fail** — Hook: "The timer got me." Gameplay: a clear timeout fail. Text overlay: countdown visible hitting 0. CTA: "This timer is brutal." Duration: 8s.
11. **Funniest fail of the week** (recurring) — Hook: "This week's funniest fail." Gameplay: best fail from the week's recordings. Text overlay: none. CTA: "Send me yours." Duration: 10–15s.

## NEAR WINS

12. **One point away** — Hook: "One point from my best score ever." Gameplay: the final seconds of a near-record run. Text overlay: live score ticking up. CTA: "So close." Duration: 12s.
13. **Last-frame save** — Hook: "This was a 1-frame save." Gameplay: a genuinely tight clear, slowed down at the key moment. Text overlay: slow-mo callout. CTA: "Did you see that?" Duration: 10s.

## LEVEL PROGRESSION

14. **Level 1 → Level 25 montage** — Hook/Gameplay/Text/CTA: matches Concept D in `CREATIVE_LIBRARY.md` (paid version); organic cut can run longer, 30–45s, with more levels shown.
15. **"How far I got today"** (recurring daily/weekly) — Hook: "Today's run: made it to level X." Gameplay: highlight reel of the run. Text overlay: level counter. CTA: "What's your best?" Duration: 15–20s.
16. **Difficulty band showcase** — Hook: "Easy vs. Normal vs. Hard vs. Expert." Gameplay: one clip per band, same mechanic type if possible for direct comparison. Text overlay: band name per clip. CTA: "Guess which one's expert before I say." Duration: 20s.

## NEW LEVELS

17. **"New level just dropped"** (only when actually shipping new content — do not post if untrue) — Hook: "New challenge just added." Gameplay: first clean clear of the new level. Text overlay: "NEW." CTA: "Go try it." Duration: 10–15s.
18. **First reaction to a new level** — Hook: "My first attempt at the new level." Gameplay: raw, unedited first try (win or fail, both work). Text overlay: none, let the reaction be real. CTA: "Rate it 1–10." Duration: 15s.

## SECRET/DIFFICULT LEVELS

19. **"The hardest level in the game"** — Hook: "This is the hardest one. Fight me." Gameplay: expert-band level, multiple attempts shown fast-cut. Text overlay: attempt counter. CTA: "Prove me wrong." Duration: 15–20s.
20. **"Nobody's cleared this yet"** (only if genuinely true — check any available data before claiming this) — Hook: framed as a community record. Gameplay: your best attempt. Text overlay: "0 clears so far." CTA: "Be the first." Duration: 15s.

## SPEEDRUNS

21. **Full-run speedrun attempt** — Hook: "Speedrunning One More Level." Gameplay: full run, sped up 2x in edit except key moments. Text overlay: running timer. CTA: "Beat my time." Duration: 30–45s.
22. **Single-level speedrun** — Hook: "Fastest clear of level 9?" Gameplay: one level, real-time. Text overlay: timer. CTA: "Send your time." Duration: 8–10s.

## PLAYER CHALLENGES

23. **"Try this and tell me your score"** — Hook: direct challenge to viewers. Gameplay: one specific level, clearly identified (e.g. "Level 11"). Text overlay: level number, large. CTA: "Comment your score." Duration: 10–15s.
24. **Duet/stitch bait** — Hook: intentionally brutal level, framed as a dare. Gameplay: your attempt (win or fail). Text overlay: "Stitch this with your attempt." CTA: explicit stitch/duet ask. Duration: 10s.
25. **Community leaderboard shoutout** (once any informal leaderboard/community exists) — Hook: "Top scores this week." Gameplay: quick cuts of top-performing clips (with credit/permission). Text overlay: names/scores. CTA: "Get on the board next week." Duration: 20s.

## DEVELOPER POV

26. **"Building a mini-challenge from scratch"** — Hook: "Here's how I designed this level." Gameplay: screen recording of the actual level logic/tuning if screen-shareable, or a narrated walkthrough of the finished level. Text overlay: process notes. CTA: "What should I build next?" Duration: 30–45s.
27. **"A day in the life of a solo dev"** — Hook: relatable solo-dev framing. Gameplay: real work (coding, testing, Play Console screens with sensitive info cropped). Text overlay: captions on what's happening. CTA: "Follow the build." Duration: 30s.
28. **Balancing decisions explained** — Hook: "Why level 20 is harder than level 19 (on purpose)." Gameplay: side-by-side of the two levels. Text overlay: the actual difficulty-curve logic in plain language. CTA: "Too hard or just right?" Duration: 20s.

## BEHIND THE SCENES

29. **Icon/brand design process** — Hook: "How I designed the app icon." Gameplay: before/after of the icon iterations (the corrected vs. double-rounded version from this repo's asset history is a genuine real example). Text overlay: process steps. CTA: "Which version's better?" Duration: 15–20s.
30. **Play Store submission process** — Hook: "Everything that has to happen before an app goes live." Gameplay: (sanitized) screens of the submission flow. Text overlay: step callouts. CTA: "Follow for the launch." Duration: 30s.

## UPDATES

31. **Update announcement** — Hook: "Here's what's new." Gameplay: the actual new feature/level/fix in action. Text overlay: changelog-style bullet list. CTA: "Update now." Duration: 15–20s.
32. **"You asked, I built it"** — Hook: ties a specific piece of community feedback to a shipped change. Gameplay: the change in action. Text overlay: quote the original request. CTA: "What's next?" Duration: 15s.

## UI IMPROVEMENTS

33. **Before/after UI comparison** — Hook: "Old UI vs. new UI." Gameplay: side-by-side or quick cut between versions. Text overlay: "OLD / NEW." CTA: "Better, right?" Duration: 10–15s.

## NEW FEATURES

34. **Feature teaser** — Hook: "Something new is coming." Gameplay: a deliberately partial/blurred preview. Text overlay: "Coming soon." CTA: "Guess what it is." Duration: 8–10s.

---

## Production notes

- Every concept above uses **real, existing game mechanics** — nothing here requires a feature that doesn't exist in `src/`. Concepts 17, 20, 26–34 are explicitly gated on real events (a real new level, a real dev session, a real update) — do not post a "new level" or "update" video about something that hasn't shipped.
- Log which concepts actually get produced and how they perform using the same repurposing/weekly-review workflow as the paid creative system — see `weekly-review.md`.
