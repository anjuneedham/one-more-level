# Hook Library

Reusable opening lines (first 1–2 seconds of any clip — the only part that determines whether someone keeps watching). Grouped by the same 5 angles as `CREATIVE_LIBRARY.md`, plus a running list for future batches. Add new hooks here as they're written; move ones that get tested into the relevant creative concept file with results.

## Challenge
- "You have ONE MORE LEVEL. Can you beat it?"
- "Bet you can't clear this in one try."
- "This is level 14. Most people don't get here."

## Curiosity
- "This level looks easy..."
- "Watch what happens at 0:07."
- "I didn't see that coming either."

## Rage / Failure
- "I was ONE jump away..."
- "3 lives. Gone in 40 seconds."
- "POV: level 14 has personally wronged you."

## Progression
- "Level 1 → Level 25"
- "Watch 25 levels in 20 seconds."
- "This is what level 1 looks like. This is what level 25 looks like."

## Skill
- "How many attempts would this take you?"
- "Be honest. First try?"
- "Comment your guess before you try it."

## Untested / backlog (write, don't launch without a concept + script first)
- "Rating every mini-game in this app."
- "I finally beat my own high score."
- "This or that: which mini-game type is worse?"
