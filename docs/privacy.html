ONE MORE LEVEL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy explains what One More Level ("the app", package com.onemorelevel.myapp) does with information when you play it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

There is no account, no sign-in and no server. The app itself doesn't collect anything about you. The one exception is the ads shown between runs and on the rewarded "continue" button, which are served by Google AdMob - see Advertising below for exactly what that involves.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of game state locally, using your device's standard app storage:

  • your best level and best score
  • your coin total
  • your sound and haptics preferences
  • a count of how many runs you have played, and when you last played

None of this is personal information, none of it identifies you, and none of it is sent anywhere. It is readable only by the app itself. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android's app settings, deletes all of it permanently. There is no backup copy, which also means a deleted best score cannot be restored.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a short video ad between some runs, and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to continue a run after losing your last life. Watching a rewarded ad is always your choice - nothing requires it.

To serve and measure these ads, AdMob's SDK can access your device's advertising ID and other device and app information (such as your general approximate location from your IP address, and technical details like device model and OS version), and sends this to Google. This app doesn't see or store that data itself - it flows directly between your device and Google. Google's own use of it is governed by Google's Ads Privacy & Terms, not by this app.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and generally limit ad personalisation, in your device's own settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, wording varies by device and OS version).

What the app itself does not do

  • It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • It does not read your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • It does not contain analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • Aside from what AdMob does on its own behalf (above), it does not sell or share any information with anyone.

Permissions

The app requests these Android permissions, and only these:

  • Internet. Needed to load ads and to let optional future features work. Without a network connection the game still plays fully offline; you'll just see "Ad unavailable" instead of an ad.
  • Vibrate. Used for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID (Android 13+). Required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve and measure ads, as described above.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 itself; if the app is used by a child, ads are requested with non-personalised settings appropriate for that context, consistent with Google's AdMob policies for child-directed treatment.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google collects its own information about downloads, purchases and crashes under Google's Privacy Policy, independently of this app. That processing is Google's, not ours, and this policy does not cover it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds analytics, a new ad format, or any other feature that changes what data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, the "last updated" date above will change, and the app's Google Play Data safety information will be updated to match.
