ONE MORE LEVEL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This policy explains what One More Level ("the app", package com.onemorelevel.game) does with information when you play it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version

The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. Everything the app remembers stays on your device and never leaves it.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of game state locally, using your device's standard app storage:

  • your best level and best score
  • your coin total
  • your sound and haptics preferences
  • a count of how many runs you have played, and when you last played

None of this is personal information, none of it identifies you, and none of it is sent anywhere. It is readable only by the app itself. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android's app settings, deletes all of it permanently. There is no backup copy, which also means a deleted best score cannot be restored.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • It does not read your contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • It does not use cookies or web tracking technologies.
  • It does not show advertising.
  • It does not contain analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not sell or share any information with anyone, because it has none to share.

Permissions

The app requests two Android permissions, and only these two:

  • Internet. Requested so that optional features can be enabled in a future version without a new permission prompt. The current version plays entirely offline and makes no network requests.
  • Vibrate. Used for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no information is collected at all, there is nothing to request the deletion of.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google collects its own information about downloads, purchases and crashes under Google's Privacy Policy, independently of this app. That processing is Google's, not ours, and this policy does not cover it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds advertising, analytics or any other feature that handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, the "last updated" date above will change, and the app's Google Play Data safety information will be updated to match.
