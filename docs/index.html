Clear a challenge, level up, get another one instantly. Tap fast, don't tap at all, repeat the pattern, dodge, swipe, balance, escape. Miss one and you lose a life. Three lives per run, and the only question that matters: how far can you get?

Plays offline. No account, no sign-in, no data collected. Your best level and best score stay on your device.

How it works

  • Each challenge lasts a few seconds and explains itself in one line.
  • Difficulty grows with your level — more objects, smaller targets, longer patterns, tighter timing — not just faster.
  • Challenges never repeat back to back, so no two runs feel the same.
  • Earn coins for every challenge you clear.
